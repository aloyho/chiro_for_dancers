The process

From first visit to
back on the floor

No guesswork. No "let's see how it feels in a few weeks." Here's exactly what happens.

01

Specific Assessment

I look at how you move, not just where it hurts. The Gonstead approach includes postural analysis, motion palpation, and X-rays — assessed through a dance performance lens, not a standard clinical one.

02

Precise Adjustment

Every adjustment targets a specific vertebra or joint — the one that's actually causing the problem. Gonstead is known for being more specific than most chiropractic methods. That precision matters when your body is your instrument.

03

Movement & Recovery Guidance

You'll get practical, dance-specific guidance between sessions — how to warm up more intelligently, what to modify while healing, and how to reinforce what we've worked on in the clinic.

04

Return to Full Performance

The goal is never just "pain gone." It's confident, unrestricted movement — back in the cypher, on stage, in class — without constantly guarding. We track progress until you're genuinely back.
